# BOOKED AF — Product Standard (approved direction, 2026-10-26)

## Promise
GET BOOKED. MAKE MONEY. HAVE A LIFE.

BOOKED AF is not another course library or a collection of reminders. It is an evidence-led career decision system: identify a costly business constraint, recommend one credible intervention, make execution easy, then measure what changed.

## Voice — every surface
Dorothy Zbornak meets Julia Sugarbaker meets Dollar Shave Club: direct, clever, specific, technically credible and dryly funny. No motivational poster language, no corporate jargon, no condescension, no filler, no profanity in public copy. Humor earns attention; precise expertise earns trust. Never repeat the same joke throughout the journey.

## A recommendation is publishable only if it has
1. A claim tied to an answer the customer gave, clearly distinguishing evidence from inference.
2. A non-obvious, realistic business mechanism: why this intervention might improve bookings, retained earnings or time.
3. A service/lane-aware implementation with real language or calculations, not a vague chore.
4. A measurable short experiment and a decision rule for what to do with the result.
5. No fabricated economics, appointments, service capabilities, client demand, or marketing permissions.
6. No contradictions with workdays, not-taking-clients status, employer constraints or existing answers.
7. A clearly superior user experience over generic advice: if the same passage works unchanged for every customer, reject it.

## Product ladder
- **Free Breakdown:** One high-quality diagnosis and one complete, useful tactical experiment. No fake scarcity or intentionally weakened advice. Email capture and on-screen results must agree.
- **Your Next 30 ($49 one time):** Coordinated four-week plan, tools, scripts, financial and capacity analysis, decision checkpoints, scenarios and tradeoffs. Preserve current purchase restrictions until verified.
- **Membership (target $40/month / $400/year):** New relevant decisions based on actual outcomes and refreshed context. Never charge for recycled generic tips.

## Signature modules to develop and validate
- **Money Autopsy:** true service economics after costs/time/commission, not only ticket prices.
- **Calendar Crime Scene:** opportunities and constraints in appointment timing, overlapping processing and boundaries, with quality and staffing guardrails.
- **Client Pipeline Investigation:** separate discovery, inquiry, booking, repeat visit and salon referrals before prescribing marketing.
- **What-If Machine:** transparent side-by-side scenarios; surface assumptions and uncertainty rather than guaranteeing savings or earnings.

## Beta acceptance gates
- Contrast tests: pairs of answer profiles with materially different constraints must receive meaningfully different advice, not just swapped headlines.
- Editorial review: each response passes the seven standards above and teaches something useful.
- No phantom facts (especially “not accepting clients” inferred from empty calendar).
- Browser walkthroughs of all stages, mobile and desktop, email capture, recovery and share/download.
- Emailed recommendations and rendered recommendations match from the same saved answers.
- Build from source, run tests, verify deploy and cache version, confirm the live domain. Keep paid checkout disabled until paid experience is launch ready.
- No launch claim or customer outreach without actual verification and explicit customer communication approval.

## Commercial discipline
Ten thousand $49 purchases would be $490,000 gross one-time revenue, **not** profit or membership revenue. Conversion, retention, distribution and acquisition costs are hypotheses to test. Collect beta feedback about clarity, perceived novelty, action taken and measurable outcomes.
