## Your Next 30: career-specific questions and useful takeaways

Ten career paths. Seven work-specific questions per path, plus common routing questions. A single-career path has 11–14 questions depending on priority; selecting several careers adds the main-career choice. Matching free answers shorten the new questions. All intake uses choices. Exact numbers are optional tools after the plan.

Each completed plan includes a first move, what to stop, what to watch, four weekly missions, three scripts, a six-point work checklist, money tools, three before-and-after measures, actions for every career answer, and help when progress stalls. Customers can save separate plans for different careers under the same purchase.

## Compatibility and limits

Legacy free-question definitions remain for saved old plans and email compatibility. This pass does not force those customers into a new diagnostic.

The active paid experience now uses the career-specific engine. Legacy paid definitions remain as a fallback but are not the route loaded by the current site.

Plans and scorecards are stored in the browser session. The download/import flow lets a customer return on another device using their original purchase link. There is no account sync. File imports preserve saved career plans and discard invalid answers. Editing a task does not inherit the completion mark from a different task.

The value check uses the customer’s entered change in money kept, subtracts extra implementation costs and the purchase price, and accepts a negative result. It is a measurement aid; it does not prove causation or promise income. Time saved is tracked separately.
