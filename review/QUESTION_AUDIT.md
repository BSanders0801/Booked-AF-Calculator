# BOOKED AF question and voice review

Reviewed against BOOKED_AF_LANGUAGE_BIBLE.md and Bradley’s instructions: short, clear, useful, with the attitude intact. This is a source and automated-flow review; live visual inspection has not been completed.

## Changes

- Removed the unused free pay-style question and the repeated paid “biggest problem” question.
- Ask outside-chair workdays only when working on time. Skip the income question when the only selection is not taking clients.
- Reuse matching pay, client-day, specialty and savings answers in Your Next 30. Show a summary and an edit control; do not overwrite existing paid answers.
- Let people choose multiple client-finding methods in Your Next 30. Carry those selections into the plan.
- Tighten question wording and answer instructions. Cover gaps in appointment-duration, savings and payment-delay choices; remove overlap in marketing budgets.
- Make the survey comment box explicitly optional and replace five writing prompts with one.
- Preserve manager identity and all selected concerns in email delivery. Keep browser and email question logic identical.
- Give free salon owners and managers contextual question wording and distinct advice.
