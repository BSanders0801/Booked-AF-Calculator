# BOOKED AF — Homepage Creative Direction (review draft)
Date: 2026-10-11
Status: PROPOSAL ONLY — not published. Do not modify production or purchase flows without separate approval and end-to-end tests.

## Principle
Brand attitude at the top, purchase clarity throughout. ONE primary action on the homepage, with a visible explanation of the paid product. Keep all existing product logic and paid framework intact.

## Header
BOOKED AF (existing logo)
Right-side text link: MEET BRADLEY
Single primary navigation action: GET MY BREAKDOWN
Existing customer access / My Plans should be available to returning users without crowding the first-time experience.

## 1 — Hero
Eyebrow: CAREER EDUCATION FOR HAIRDRESSERS
Headline:
FULLY BOOKED.
COMPLETELY MISERABLE.
Deadpan: Congratulations. You've really made it.
Clarifier: We help hairdressers figure out what's working, what's costing them, and what to do next — without giving their entire lives to the salon.
Primary button: SHOW ME MY BREAKDOWN →
Microcopy: Free. About 3 minutes. See what needs your attention first.

OPTIONAL ALTERNATE TO TEST (not concurrent headline):
YOUR CHAIR IS A BUSINESS.
YOUR LIFE IS NOT THE OVERHEAD.
The existing headline is approved brand language and must not be discarded without a side-by-side review.

## 2 — The immediate value proposition (maximum 3 lines)
Section headline: YOUR BOOK IS FULL. YOUR BANK ACCOUNT DISAGREES.
Body: More appointments aren't always the answer. First, find out where your time, money, and clients are actually going.
Small label: Start with your free Breakdown. Get a useful first step, not another motivational speech.

## 3 — How it works
Headline: THREE MINUTES. SOME VERY USEFUL ANSWERS.
1. Tell us how you actually work.
2. See what needs attention first.
3. Decide what you're going to do about it.
Important: Verify against real Breakdown screens before publishing. Do not promise individualized financial outcomes.

## 4 — Paid offer, clearly explained
Eyebrow: WHEN YOU'RE READY TO DO SOMETHING ABOUT IT
Headline: ADVICE IS NICE. A PLAN IS BETTER.
Product name: YOUR NEXT 30
Price: $49 ONE-TIME
Body: Four weeks of focused actions, usable scripts, and simple tools built around your kind of work.
Link: SEE WHAT'S IN THE $49 PLAN →
Do not claim immediate access, delivery timelines, or personalization beyond verified functionality. Existing Breakdown-to-paid flow and gating must be checked before wiring CTA.

## 5 — Founder credibility
Eyebrow: MEET THE PERSON WHO'S MADE THE MISTAKES
Headline: GREAT HAIR. EXPENSIVE LESSONS.
Copy: Nearly 30 years as a colorist, salon owner, and educator. I've made money, worked too much, and learned some very expensive lessons. BOOKED AF exists so other hairdressers can skip a few of them.
Use existing founder photo, unaltered.
Text link: MORE ABOUT BRADLEY →

## 6 — Final action
Headline: YOUR CAREER OWES YOU A LIFE.
Copy: Let's see what yours is actually giving you.
Primary button: SHOW ME MY BREAKDOWN →
Small secondary text link: Want the full plan? See Your Next 30 — $49.

## Footer
About, privacy, terms, customer access, support. Do not hide necessary legal/payment information.

## Remove from HOME only — do not delete source content
- All three detailed earnings examples => existing sample/details pages
- Career selector with five tabs => Breakdown/sample page
- Ten-career-paths teaser => resource page
- Long founder biography => About
- Duplicative start/paid offer cards => one paid introduction only
- Student starter link => relevant secondary page, not hero

## Approval checklist
- 5-second user test: who, why, where to click
- Test hero A (provocation) vs existing headline B
- Validate free Breakdown duration and promises
- Confirm $49 product description and buyer experience
- Verify navigation on desktop and iPhone
- Test payment in sandbox / Stripe test mode only
- Keep production unchanged until approval and tests succeed

## Brand review
Keep black/white/hot pink. No stock images, no retouched founder imagery, no beige coaching design. Humor targets industry nonsense, never customers.
